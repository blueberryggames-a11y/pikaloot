Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

What we collect

When you upload a file, we store the file and basic metadata (filename, size, upload time) on Firebase. We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.

When visitors unlock a file, their interaction is logged (unlock count) but no personal data is stored by us.

Third-party services

We use Firebase (Google) for file storage and database. Firebase may collect usage data per their own privacy policy.

We use CPA networks (such as CPAGrip or AdWorkMedia) to display offers on unlock pages. These third parties have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or tracking technologies.

Cookies

Our site itself does not set cookies. Third-party services (Firebase, CPA networks) may set their own cookies.

File content

We do not review or share the files you upload. Files are stored securely and only accessible via the unique link you receive. Do not upload illegal content — it will be removed and the link disabled.
